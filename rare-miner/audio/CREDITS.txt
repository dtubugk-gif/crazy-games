כורה נדיר (Rare Miner) - audio credits
======================================

Music
-----
music_title.ogg, music_dig.ogg, music_deep.ogg, stinger_results.ogg, stinger_perfect.ogg: original music, composed and
synthesised in code for this game (marimba, kalimba, vibraphone, glockenspiel, plucked guitar, flute, bass, pads, brushes and
shaker are all generated - no samples, no library loops). All in D at 96 BPM; music_dig and music_deep share the same 24 bars
of harmony and are exactly 60.00 s long, so they can be crossfaded in sync. Rebuild: art/audio-work/compose.mjs (+ synth.mjs).

Sound effects and ambience
--------------------------
Designed for this game from recordings in the Sonniss GDC 2026 Game Audio Bundle (royalty-free, commercial use,
no credit required; media production only - the sounds may not be used for AI/ML training): cut, layered, pitched,
EQ-ed and given a little generated room / cave reverb. The tonal parts of the ore, streak, timer and UI sounds are recorded
music boxes, xylophone and kalimba notes pitched onto D-major notes. The cave drips and lava blubs are recorded water
bubbles, pitched and placed in a generated cave reverb (the library has no drip recordings). No synthesised layers in the SFX.
Rebuild: art/audio-work/build_all.mjs.
