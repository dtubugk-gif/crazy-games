סערת כוחות (Power Storm) - audio credits
========================================

Music
-----
music_title.ogg, music_level.ogg, music_boss.ogg, music_win.ogg, music_lose.ogg: original music, composed and synthesised
in code for this game (brass, strings, harp, timpani, percussion, choir are all generated - no samples, no library loops).
Rebuild: art/audio-work/compose.mjs (+ orch.mjs, synth.mjs).

Sound effects and ambience
--------------------------
Designed for this game from recordings in the Sonniss GDC 2026 Game Audio Bundle (royalty-free, commercial use,
no credit required; media production only - the sounds may not be used for AI/ML training): cut, layered, pitched,
EQ-ed and given a little generated room reverb. Synthesised glue (always under the recordings): sub-bass weight under the
biggest impacts, the steam-whistle chord in boss_roar, a falling blip in energy_empty, the rising shimmer in charge_loop and
generator_on, the high icy partials in ice_beam_loop, and the pigeon coos (amb_roof) and distant gulls (amb_canal), which the
library has no recordings of. Rebuild: art/audio-work/build_all.mjs.
