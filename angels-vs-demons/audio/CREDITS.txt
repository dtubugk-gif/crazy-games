מלאכים נגד שדים (Angels vs Demons) - audio credits
==================================================

Music
-----
music_title, music_scan, music_battle_angel, music_battle_demon, music_boss, stinger_victory, stinger_defeat (.ogg + m4a/*.mp4):
original music, composed and synthesised in code for this game - brass, strings, harp, timpani, percussion, glockenspiel,
flute and choir are all generated (no samples, no library loops). Rebuild: art/audio-work/compose.mjs (+ orch.mjs, synth.mjs).

Sound effects and ambience
--------------------------
Designed for this game from recordings in the Sonniss GDC 2026 Game Audio Bundle (royalty-free, commercial use,
no credit required; media production only - the sounds may not be used for AI/ML training): cut, layered, pitched,
EQ-ed and given a little generated room reverb. Creature voices are pitched and blended into kid-friendly cartoon monsters.
Synthesised glue (always under the recordings): sub-bass weight under the biggest impacts, the choir chords in verdict_angel,
verdict_demon, special_holy, shield_bless and boss_roar_angel, the horn call in wave_start, a bow-string pluck in arrow_shoot,
the falling whistle in meteor_fall, glockenspiel notes in scan_beep / verdict_angel / special_holy, the wind-chime notes in
amb_heaven, the singing-bowl hum in amb_scan and the sub thump of low_hp. Rebuild: art/audio-work/build_all.mjs.
