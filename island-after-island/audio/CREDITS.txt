אי אחרי אי (Island after Island) - audio credits
================================================

Music
-----
music_explore.ogg, music_danger.ogg, music_win.ogg: original music, composed and synthesised in code for this game
(no samples, no loops from libraries). Rebuild: art/audio-work/compose.mjs.

no credit required; media production only - the sounds may not be used for AI/ML training), cut, layered, pitched,
